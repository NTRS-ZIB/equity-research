Basis

  1. Which items are genuinely scheduled, and which are event-driven and could land any day. Only one item here has a company-announced date.
  2. Timing confidence rates the date, never the outcome. A High-confidence catalyst can still be a coin flip.
  3. Estimated dates are cadence-based until the company announces. Treat them as approximate.
  4. Bitdeer reports as a foreign private issuer. It files an annual report on Form 20-F and furnishes interim information on Form 6-K at its own cadence, with no quarterly report to a statutory deadline and no proxy statement. That is why this calendar carries fewer scheduled items than a domestic filer's would: most of the regular obligations that anchor a calendar do not apply.

D3

Arrangement of the Tydal letters of credit

Two-sided
Timing before Tydal rent commencement, date not announced
Impact High
Confidence Low
Provenance Open

Bitdeer states that a credit backstop of approximately $1.3B is anticipated to be arranged by affiliates of J.P. Morgan and another top-tier global financial institution via letters of credit, subject to customary conditions. Until that is confirmed, approximately $4.7B of contracted revenue rests on a tenant that is a subsidiary of Volta, supported by credit that is expected rather than in place. This is dated rather than standing because it must logically be settled before rent begins, but no date is announced and it may be disclosed only when it happens.

Upside

Confirmation that approximately $1.3B of letters of credit is in place converts the Tydal lease from a contracted amount into credit-supported revenue, and materially changes what the $4.7B figure means.

Downside

The letters are arranged on narrower terms, at a smaller amount, or not at all. The word anticipated is doing real work in the announcement, and a silence extending for many months would itself be information.

Analyst assumption

Placing this in the 6 to 12 month band is a judgement, not a company statement. Bitdeer has disclosed no delivery date for Tydal and no date for the credit arrangement. It is banded here on the reasoning that credit support is customarily settled well before a facility of this size is energised, and the confidence is marked Low to reflect that the date rests on that reasoning rather than on a document.

Source: Company release, 4 August 2026. Filed

Beyond 12 months, context onlyn/a

No structural item is carried in this band. The Tydal lease runs 16 years from a commencement date the company has not disclosed, with a one-time 8-year extension option, but no dated decision point inside that term is stated.

Building and delivering the 121 megawatts at Tydal

Two-sided
Timing Ongoing
Impact High
Why undated No delivery date disclosed in the announcement

The lease commits 121 IT megawatts, supported by an estimated 133 gross megawatts, at a stated power usage effectiveness of approximately 1.1 on 100 percent renewable energy. None of it is built. S5

Bitcoin price exposure through self-mining

Two-sided
Timing Ongoing
Impact High
Why undated A market exposure, repriced continuously
Provenance Filed

Bitdeer's own risk disclosure states that, in its self-mining business, a decline in bitcoin prices would directly reduce profitability, as mining rewards would be worth less while operating costs such as electricity and equipment continue. This is the exposure that separates Bitdeer from a pure colocation lessor, and it cuts both ways: the 2025 return to a $65.6M profit came in a period when the mining businesses recovered alongside a 77 percent rise in revenue.

Upside

A rising bitcoin price lifts self-mining revenue against a largely fixed cost base, and also lifts demand for the SEALMINER hardware the company sells to other operators.

Downside

A falling price compresses self-mining margin and hardware demand at the same time, since both depend on the same cycle. The filings read here disclose no hedge.

Source: Form 20-F, 30 April 2026. Filed

05Dilution & capital overlay

Sits across every other catalyst rather than beside them.

Bitdeer's capital overlay has an unusual feature: a reader cannot state how much dilution has already happened, because no filed document gives a current share count.

The last stated count is seven months old. 235,552,084 ordinary shares at 31 December 2025, being 191,152,162 Class A and 44,399,922 Class V. That figure excludes 3,364,711 Class A shares repurchased but not cancelled and 7,258,176 reserved under the share incentive plans, with a further 3,404,713 reserved and unissued.

At least 7,439,094 further Class A shares were issued by late February 2026. 1,936,064 under the at-the-market programme between 1 January and 23 February for $28.2M gross, and 5,503,030 in the registered direct offering of $43,694,058 at $7.94. Five months after that are unaccounted for. On those two disclosed issues alone the count reaches at least 242,991,178.

The at-the-market programme is a standing facility, not a completed raise. Twelve agents are named under the sales agreement dated 3 January 2025, and stock can be issued at any time without announcement. Ten of those twelve firms also publish research on the company, which is set out in the research report.

At least $1.15B of convertible principal sits above the equity. Three issues whose amounts are established: $375.0M of 4.875% notes due 2031 priced June 2025, $400.0M of 4.00% notes due 2031 priced November 2025, and $375.0M of 5.00% notes due 2032. A fourth issue, due 2029 and priced November 2024, has a principal this document did not establish. Conversion of any of it adds to a count already unknown.

Analyst assumption

The reading taken here is that the market capitalisation stated in the research report is a floor, and that the true figure is at least $2,765M on the two disclosed issues alone rather than the $2,681M the last filed count produces. This is arithmetic on filed figures, not a disclosed number, and it does not attempt to estimate issuance after February 2026. The second-quarter results on 10 August (D1) are the obvious opportunity for the company to close the gap described in the standing condition on the share count (S3).

Source: Form 20-F, 30 April 2026; Form 424B5, 23 February 2026. Filed

08Methodology & confidence scale

Provenance tags

TagWhat it asserts
FiledStated in an SEC filing or company release, cited by form and date. Also covers a dated official publication by a named non-SEC issuer, with the issuer named in the Source line.
EstimateDerived or inferred here. The arithmetic is shown.
OpenExpected but unconfirmed. Nothing filed either way.
MarketPrice, volume, float, published targets and ratings. Stamped with the close or publication date.
PressReported by a named publication that is neither the issuer nor an analyst, cited by outlet and date. Corroborates; never the sole basis for a material claim.
SocialPublicly posted by a named account, cited by handle and date. Asserts that the statement was made, never that it is true.

Timing confidence

LevelMeans
HighDate is company-announced, protocol-defined, or statutorily fixed.
MediumDate inferred from filing cadence or a stated deadline window.
LowDate is a judgement call. Could move by a quarter or more.

Confidence rates timing, never outcome. A High-confidence catalyst can be a coin flip; a Low-confidence one can be near-certain in direction.

Impact is independent of confidence. A High-impact, Low-confidence item is the most important kind here: it matters enormously and could land any time.
