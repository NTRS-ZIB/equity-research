The gating chain

The gate opened and the chain did not move, which is the single most important finding in this revision. The old chain ran: the Bakkt Agent launch gates partner activation, which gates volume against the year-end target (D4), which gates retained revenue at the quarterly reports, which is what would make equity issuance optional rather than necessary. The rails went live in September (R9) and volume fell for a third consecutive quarter, to $147.5M. So the dependency was real and the gate was not the binding constraint.

What binds instead is three things, and only one of them is a catalyst. Volume: the year-end target (D4) needs 13.2 times the Q3 quarter in Q4, and the first monthly report (D1) will show the trajectory in December rather than in March. Cash: $37.3M against a $17.9M quarterly burn, a going-concern forecast that counts $17M of litigation proceeds (S2), a $27.8M India call (D10), and a disclosed commitment of principal capital to counterparty credit (S4). And the price: at $2.75 the goodwill impairment trigger the company names has already occurred (D8), and every dollar raised under the at-the-market program costs roughly three times the shares it would have in September (S1). The chain now runs from cash rather than from product.
