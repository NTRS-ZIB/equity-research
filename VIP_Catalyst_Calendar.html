D1

Schedule 14C information statement

Two-sided
Timing Definitive statement pending, no date announced
Impact Medium
Confidence Medium
Provenance Open

The preliminary statement was filed on August 5, 2026, sixteen days after the 8-K. It gives notice of action by written consent of majority stockholders controlled by Atlas Capital Resources GP LLC, the consent dated July 19, 2026 against a record date of July 17, 2026.

The clock has not started, and that is the distinction that matters here. What was filed is the preliminary statement, which is subject to an SEC review period before the definitive one is mailed. The consent becomes effective 20 calendar days after the definitive statement is first furnished to shareholders, and the PIPE transaction (D7) cannot close until it is. So the buffer in front of the 10 October outside date cannot be computed from the August 5 date, and treating it as the start of the twenty-day clock would be wrong in the company’s favour. The next observable is the DEF 14C, which has not been filed.

What the statement discloses. Two actions were approved: the PIPE issuance, for Nasdaq Listing Rule 5635(b), covering 17,146,190 Class A shares, a $10.0M senior secured convertible promissory note and a three-year warrant over 1,754,386 shares; and the equity plan increase for Rule 5635(c). At the record date there were 15,400,548 Class A and 2,733,394 Class B shares outstanding, so the PIPE’s Class A issuance alone exceeds the entire Class A count. The Majority Stockholders held 1,505,351 Class A and 2,680,031 Class B, approximately 23.1% of capital and 66.2% of the total voting power. It does not disclose the nature of the Regulatory Approvals, which this card expected it to and which the 8-K also left unspecified.

Upside

A definitive statement filed promptly after SEC review still leaves the twenty-day period inside the outside date. The preliminary filing removes the reading that the company was sitting on it.

Downside

Each day of delay compresses the buffer in front of a hard maturity. The company controls when it files the definitive statement but not how long the review takes, so from here the delay is only partly within its gift. The Regulatory Approvals remain unspecified.

Source: Preliminary Information Statement on Schedule 14C, filed August 5, 2026; Form 8-K, July 20, 2026, Items 5.07 and Additional Information. Filed

S10

Title V compliance and coal ash remediation

Two-sided
Timing Ongoing to 2028 and 2030
Impact Medium
Why undated Staged obligations rather than a single event
Provenance Filed

The November 2025 settlement resolved a four-year dispute and secured a final Title V permit, which is real de-risking.
