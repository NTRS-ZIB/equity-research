The contract that prices the input to 70% of revenue runs out in five weeks. The Form 10-Q states that the current power supply agreement for the Midland facility will expire December 31, 2026, that the Company is currently evaluating its strategy relating to the Midland Facility, and that in the short term the Company expects to use one-month forward price contracts to manage its electrical needs.

The expiring agreement is the June 2022 power supply agreement with Dynegy, formerly Energy Harbor, for delivery of a fixed portion of the facility's electricity at a fixed price through December 2026, with any excess charged at a newly quoted price. It is accounted for as a derivative rather than as a normal purchase, because the Company can sell the power back to the grid instead of taking delivery, so it is carried at fair value with the change running through earnings each period.

Two things change at once and they pull in opposite directions. Energy management revenue of $6.06M in Q3, 70% of the total, comes from curtailing load and selling excess capacity at this facility, which is worth more when power prices are high. Cost of revenues is primarily direct power costs, which is worse when power prices are high. The fixed-price contract has been standing between the two. Replacing it with one-month forward contracts moves both legs onto a monthly repricing basis.

Upside

Monthly contracting at a facility whose revenue rises with power prices can be worth more than a fixed price, and a company still evaluating its strategy for the site has not committed itself to a long contract at an unattractive moment.

Downside

A short-dated procurement basis replaces a four-and-a-half year fixed price at the facility that generates almost all current revenue, at a company with $3.1M of cash, and the derivative that has been smoothing reported earnings stops doing so.

Analyst assumption

No replacement arrangement has been announced, and the Form 10-Q describes the forward contracts as a short-term expectation rather than a commitment. Whether the strategy evaluation reaches a longer contract, a different site, or a disposal is not in the record.
