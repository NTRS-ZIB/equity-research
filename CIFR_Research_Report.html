02Business model

Cipher Digital originates power, secures grid interconnection, builds data centers on the resulting sites, and leases them to hyperscalers on long-term triple-net terms. It describes itself as vertically integrated across land and power origination, interconnection, site development, design and construction, and ongoing operations.

Bitcoin mining is what the sites do until a tenant needs them. That is the clearest single fact about this company: mining is not a second business run alongside the leasing business, it is the interim use of the same electricity. Where a lease is signed, the miners come out. Mining at Black Pearl began in June 2025, ramped to 150 MW by September 2025, and ceased in February 2026 because Amazon had leased the site.

The rebrand from Cipher Mining to Cipher Digital on February 20, 2026 is the company's own statement of that shift, made in the same month it switched off a mining site and sold its joint-venture mining interests.

What the lease revenue is, and what it is not

The $5.852 million is $5.231 million of fixed base rent and $0.621 million of variable payments, the latter being operating expenses, mostly power, provided to the tenant at cost and recorded gross in both revenue and cost of revenue. Cost of revenue for the HPC segment was $0.726 million, so the segment's gross margin in its first quarter is 87.6 per cent, which is consistent with the roughly 100 per cent net operating income margin the company describes for Stingray.

The cash is arriving faster than the revenue, and the income statement shows the slower half. Against $5.852 million recognised, the balance sheet carries $46.185 million of deferred revenue, described as a deferred rent liability, and $1.4 million of accrued rent receivable. A deferred rent liability arises where cash billed exceeds straight-line recognition. So roughly eight times the recognised figure has been billed and is being released over the lease term. Neither number is wrong and the recognised one is the less flattering, which is the one the headline uses.

What replaces mining is contracted and mostly not yet earning. 04Industry & market backdrop

Power and interconnection are the binding constraints on AI infrastructure, and the converted bitcoin miners hold both. Cipher's own framing is a power-first sourcing process, beginning up to a few years before planned development and advancing sites through the interconnection queue to energisation.

Texas is the center of gravity for this company. Ten of its eleven sites are in Texas and operate within the Electric Reliability Council of Texas market. The exception, Ulysses in Ohio, was acquired in December 2025 with capacity secured from AEP Ohio and is expected to energise in the fourth quarter of 2027, giving direct access to PJM. The company describes it as its first acquisition outside Texas and as a deliberate geographic diversification.

The Texas regulatory position hardened across the quarter and is now in a periodic report rather than only in press releases. PUCT rules implementing Senate Bill 6's large-load interconnection standards took effect on October 8, 2026; SB 6 requires the Commission to amend its wholesale transmission cost-allocation rules by December 31, 2026 and the company names a move from four to twelve coincident peak allocation, minimum demand charges on contracted peak demand and a greater large-load share of transmission upgrade costs among the measures under consideration; ERCOT has temporarily paused certain energization approvals for large data center loads pending the Batch Zero verification it expects to report by December 10, 2026; and on September 21, 2026 the Governor of Texas directed the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality to halt all permits sought by data centers in Texas until the audit completes, which the company says it does not expect to affect its near-term development timeline. In the same quarter it received a conditional Batch Zero base load classification for 1 GW at Colchis and conditional studied load classifications with Provisional Controllable Load Resource designations for a further 1.6 GW. Filed Form 10-Q, November 2, 2026, Part II Item 1A

Two features of Cipher's interconnection position are stated more precisely than is usual in this sector, and both matter. Barber Lake has received approvals for 300 MW of interconnection without any restrictions on the load profile, which is the difference between firm capacity and curtailable capacity. Black Pearl has approvals for 300 MW. Both have entered the agreements necessary to participate in the ERCOT market.

On August 10, 2026 the company published its own statement on the Governor's directive, saying it supports the audit, is committed to full transparency into its power use, water use and community impact, and had responded to the PUCT water consumption survey the Governor's letter names. It also states that it pays the costs of interconnecting its own facilities and commits to curtailing load during ERCOT grid emergencies under Senate Bill 6. This is the issuer's own characterisation and is recorded as such, not as evidence about how the audit will treat its sites. Filed Press release, August 10, 2026

On the demand side the tenant list across the sector is short, and Cipher appears twice in it. Fluidstack, backstopped by Google, is also TeraWulf's counterparty at two campuses. Amazon Data Services is Cipher's tenant at two sites. A reader following this sector will meet the same handful of names across several issuers' filings, which makes counterparty concentration a structural feature rather than a company-specific failing.

The first profitable quarter is a warrant mark

Cipher reported net income of $31.596 million for the third quarter against an operating loss of $71.915 million. What bridges them is a $127.180 million gain on the fair value of the warrant liability, which arose because the share price fell. The quarter in which the equity lost more than half its value is the quarter the company reported a profit, and those are the same fact.

The same income statement reports basic earnings of $0.08 and diluted earnings of $(0.22). That is not an error. Under the if-converted method diluted earnings per share assumes the convertibles converted at the start of the period, adding back their interest and their shares; here the combination turns a profit into a loss. A reader who takes either figure as the quarter's result will be wrong, and the company's own Adjusted EBITDA of negative $9.963 million is the more useful of the three because it strips the warrant mark, the power purchase agreement mark and share-based compensation.

Over nine months the direction reverses: a net loss of $350.249 million against $88.039 million a year earlier. The widening is not operational alone. Amortisation of debt discount and issuance costs rose by $184.9 million, of which $46.584 million is the 2031 convertible notes' pure discount accretion on a zero coupon, at an effective interest rate of 6.890 per cent. Interest expense of $184.888 million over nine months is 2.14 times the period's revenue, and $58.994 million in the quarter is 2.21 times it.
