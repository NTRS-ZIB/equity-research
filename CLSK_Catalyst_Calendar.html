S1

Sandersville completion guarantee

Down
Timing Ongoing
Impact High
Why undated Runs until completion, with no stated cap and no stated term

This is what remains of the financing condition it replaces. CleanSpark will provide a customary completion guarantee under which it funds CSDC Finance I, LLC as necessary to ensure the timely completion of the Sandersville facility, in the event that the proceeds of the notes and the available funds are insufficient to do so. The notes are non-recourse to CleanSpark for payment. They are obligations of the issuer and of CSRE Properties Sandersville, LLC, secured on those entities and on the issuer’s own equity. The completion guarantee is the one place the parent balance sheet stands behind this project, and it is therefore the whole of the residual exposure.

No cap is disclosed and none is assumed here. The Form 8-K calls the guarantee customary and states no maximum, no trigger test and no term beyond completion. Whether it is capped is not established by anything read for this revision: the indenture filed as Exhibit 4.1 runs to 1.65MB and was not read in full, and the guarantee itself was not filed as a separate exhibit.

What makes it live is the size of the build against the parent. The company’s own development cost is ~$11.9M per IT MW, or $2,082.5M against 175 MW, and shareholders’ equity was $761.3M at June 30, 2026. That is 2.7 times the equity, and a cost overrun does not fall on the noteholders.

Upside

Construction completes within the proceeds and the guarantee expires having cost nothing, which is what the company’s own illustrative model assumes.

Downside

Overruns or delay draw on a parent whose cash was $202.6M at June 30, 2026 and whose remaining liquidity is bitcoin-collateralised, at the same time as the lease milestones that carry rent abatement and termination rights.

How the reporting dates were derived

The fiscal year ends 30 September, so the reporting cadence runs a month later than a December year-end peer on the same calendar quarter. Deriving a date from a December year-end would put every estimate here roughly thirty days out. The cadence was taken from the filing record itself: quarterly reports lodged February 5, 2025, May 8, 2025, August 7, 2025, February 5, 2026 and May 11, 2026, and annual reports lodged November 25, 2025 for fiscal 2025 and, after a late notification on December 2, 2024, for fiscal 2024. As a large accelerated filer the company has forty days from a quarter end and sixty days from a year end.

Confidence rates timing, never outcome. A High-confidence catalyst can be a coin flip; a Low-confidence one can be near-certain in direction.

Impact is independent of confidence. A High-impact, Low-confidence item is the most important kind here: it matters enormously and could land any time. The Sandersville first deliveries (D7) and the Sandersville completion guarantee (S1) are both of that kind.
